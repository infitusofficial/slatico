# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial public release preparation of `@slatico/svg` for **Slatico by Infitus Creative Studio**.
- Includes all 27 Tier 1 production icon families across all 7 official styles (`189` total SVG assets and 7 style sprite sheets).
- Provides `renderSvgMarkup`, `getSvgAsset`, `getSvgSprite`, `getSpriteSymbolId`, and `scopeSvgIds` with full support for monochrome `color`, Duotone `secondaryColor` and `secondaryOpacity`, and dual-color `Color` customization (`--slatico-primary-color` and `--slatico-secondary-color`).
